# Research — Collaborators and support-slot targets

Scope: artists in the lane who are one to three tiers above a developing act — enough reach to matter, close enough to answer — with a tilt toward acts based in or regularly playing {geo_scope}. Include producers, remixers and live acts; exclude headliners (they are context, not targets, and belong in the lane definition).

Per record: `city`/`country` where they are based (published), `roster_trace` = their labels and recent notable bills, `links` (Spotify, SoundCloud, RA, Bandsintown), `summary` covering audience size band (use monthly listeners or follower counts as published, in bands: 0 <1k · 1 1–10k · 2 10–50k · 3 50–250k · 4 250k+), recent and upcoming shows in {geo_scope}, and the published way to reach them or their management. `act_level` 3 only with a published management or booking contact; 2 for an official contact form; 1 otherwise.

Target: {target_count} records.
