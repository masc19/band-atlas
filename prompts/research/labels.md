# Research — Labels

Scope: labels that have released acts in the lane in the last three years, worldwide, with a tilt toward {geo_scope}. Include artist-run boutiques, mid-size independents and the larger houses that define the lane; the latter will be `act_level` 1 and that is useful information.

Per record: `who` (founder / A&R from a published source), `roster_trace` (four to six named acts), `act_level` and `apply_how` (demo form URL, demo email policy, or "management only"), `links` (site, Bandcamp, Beatport, Spotify, SoundCloud), a neutral `summary` covering tier (boutique / mid / major-affiliated), release cadence and whether they develop unsigned acts. `min_draw_band` is not meaningful for labels; set `summary` to say what stage of artist they typically sign (demos / singles / EPs / touring acts).

Target: {target_count} records.
