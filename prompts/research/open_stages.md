# Research — Open stages

Scope: places in and within roughly two hours of **{city}** where a developing act can get a short set (10–40 minutes) through sign-up, application or a light direct pitch: open decks, open mics that allow backing tracks, application-based showcase series, promoter collectives with early slots, small rooms that book by email, radio and livestream stages, scene meetups, regional festival open calls.

Per record fill `stage`: `format`, `rig_support` (0 no stage · 1 DJ only · 2 partial — tracks and a mic · 3 full — line-in, mic, DI), `sound` (0 basic/unknown · 1 PA + subs confirmed · 2 named club system), `genre_focus` for the lane (0 general · 1 lane-friendly · 2 lane-focused), `set_minutes`, `gear`. Also `when` (cadence and next dates), `apply_how`, `min_age`, `pay_to_play`, `capacity`. Sound-system claims need a named source; mark unsourced ones in `verify`. Include closed venues as `folded` when they still appear in listings.

Three sweeps work best: (1) open mics and submission nights, (2) lane-specific showcases and open decks, (3) the surrounding region. Dedupe across sweeps by `id`.

Target: {target_count} records.
