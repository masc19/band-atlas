# Research — Bookers and talent buyers

Scope: named people who book beyond a single room in {geo_scope} — talent buyers at promoter groups, curators of multi-venue series, festival stage bookers — who program the lane. In-house desks of individual venues belong to the Venues category, not here.

Per record: `name` (person), `org`, `city`, `summary` of what they book and at what level (touring headliners only vs local support), `roster_trace` of recent bills, `min_draw_band`, `act_level`, `contact` only from a published listing (company contact page, official event listing), `contact_source`. Role currency matters: state the newest date you found the person in that role in `verify`.

Target: {target_count} records.
