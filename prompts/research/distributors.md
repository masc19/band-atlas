# Research — Distributors

Scope: digital distributors available to an unsigned act in {geo_scope}, from DIY subscription services to label-services companies that take acts by approval.

Per record fill `distribution`: `model` (subscription / per-release / revenue share / approval-only), `royalty_share` as published, `dance_store_delivery` (Beatport and Traxsource confirmed on the distributor's own partner page, not on a blog), `splits_tooling` (built-in royalty splits and whether they cost extra), `catches` (unpublished contract terms, YouTube Content ID fees, exit terms). `cost_amount` = year-one cash cost in USD for one artist with unlimited releases where that plan exists; explain the arithmetic in `cost`. `act_level` 3 for self-serve sign-up, 2 for application, 1 for invitation only.

Target: {target_count} records.
