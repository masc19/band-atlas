# Research — Live booking agents

Scope: agencies and named agents representing acts in the lane, in {geo_scope} and the international agencies that define the top of the lane. Include independents known to develop emerging acts; most records will be `reach` or `above` by nature — that is the point of the map.

Per record: `name` (agency), `who` (agents in the lane, from published rosters), `roster_trace`, `summary` on which tier they sign at and any published policy on unsigned or developing acts, `act_level` (3 published submission route, 2 contact form, 1 none), `contact` per policy. Agency restructures and rebrands are common: record the current name and note the old one in `verify`.

Target: {target_count} records.
