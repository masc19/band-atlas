# Research — Festivals

Scope: fan-facing festivals that program the lane in {geo_scope}, from boutique camps to the majors. The majors will be `act_level` 1 and group as "attend" or "above" later — include them so the map has a top.

Per record: `when` (month, dates), `month`, `deadline` for any open call, `apply_how`, `cost` and `cost_amount` for application fees, `capacity`, `roster_trace` (recent lineup names in the lane), `min_draw_band`, `summary` on who books the stages (in-house, partner promoters, agencies) and whether local or emerging acts have been booked in the last two editions. `status` folded for events that no longer run, with the source.

Target: {target_count} records.
