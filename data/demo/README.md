# Demo data

48 **real** records for New York (open stages, promoters, bookers) plus real labels, distributors, agents, festivals and showcases, researched in August–September 2026 from official pages and listings under the published-contacts-only policy. The **artist is fictional**: "The Hollow Tide", a three-piece live-electronic band from New York with no draw yet. Every judgment in the example atlas is computed from that fictional profile — nothing here is advice for a real act.

Facts carry a `seen_date`; treat anything older than six months as a lead to re-verify, not a fact. Do not contact anyone listed here on the strength of this demo alone — open the source, confirm the route, then write.

Collabs are intentionally absent: a public demo should not carry real artists' management contacts.
